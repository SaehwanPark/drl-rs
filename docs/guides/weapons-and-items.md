# Weapons & Items Catalog

**drl-rs** features an extensive arsenal of firearms, heavy ordnance, energy weapons, defensive combat armor, modification packs, and powerful artifact equipment.

---

## 🔫 Standard Firearm Armory

### 1. Pistols & Sidearms
- **Pistol**: Standard-issue 9mm sidearm. Holds 12 rounds. Fast reload (single-turn magazine swap). Supports **Aimed Fire** (+3 accuracy at double action cost).
- **Combat Pistol**: Enhanced tactical sidearm with upgraded base accuracy and rapid chambering.

### 2. Shotguns & Scatterguns
- **Standard Shotgun**: Pump-action 12-gauge scattergun. Holds 1 shell. Fires 7 pellets with 1-tile knockback displacement.
- **Combat Shotgun**: Magazine-fed tactical shotgun with a 5-shell capacity. Supports single-shell tactical loading and chamber cycling.
- **Double Shotgun**: Break-action twin-barrel shotgun. Discharges 2 shells simultaneously for 14 high-impact pellets and heavy knockback.
- **Super Shotgun**: Sawn-off twin-barrel monster. Fires devastating dual-shell volleys at close range.
- **Assault Shotgun**: Full-auto drum-fed combat shotgun with rapid clearing capabilities.

### 3. Rapid-Fire Ballistic Weapons
- **Chaingun**: 4-round burst 9mm rotary weapon. Excellent mid-range suppression against swarms. Supports a first-level 3-projectile chainfire burst.
- **Minigun**: Heavy 8-barrel rotary machine gun. Chews through ammo at 8 rounds per salvo, with first-level 6-projectile, second-level 8-projectile, and third-level 12-projectile chainfire bursts for open combat corridors.

### 4. Heavy Rocket & Explosive Launchers
- **Rocket Launcher**: High-explosive ordnance firing single rockets with a radius-2 explosive splash.
- **Missile Launcher**: Multi-tube launcher holding up to 4 rockets for continuous area-of-effect suppression.

## ⚡ Energy & Plasma Weaponry

- **Blaster**: Self-charging micro-cell energy pistol. Holds 10 energy charges; automatically regenerates charges over time. Supports Aimed Fire.
- **Laser Rifle**: Long-range beam projector firing a 5-cell precision coherent light volley with first-level 4-projectile, second-level 5-projectile, and third- and fourth-level 7-projectile chainfire bursts.
- **Plasma Rifle**: Rapid-fire energy rifle discharging 6-cell plasma bolts with thermal splash damage and first-level 4-projectile plus second-level 6-projectile chainfire bursts.
- **Nuclear Plasma Rifle**: Experimental nuclear core plasma rifle. High-yield discharge, first-level 4-projectile chainfire, and continuous internal power recharge.
- **BFG 9000**: The ultimate bio-force weapon. Fires a massive 40-cell plasma ball (radius 8 splash, massive 16-tile kinetic knockback).
- **BFG 10K**: Rapid-cycling experimental variant firing five 5-cell plasma charges with delayed schedule explosions; its first-level chainfire emits four exact-hit charges for 20 cells.

## 🔮 Exotic & Unique Artifacts

- **Trigun**: Legendary triple-barrel handgun. Supports **Alt-Reload** for a high-risk, catastrophic level-clearing energy detonation.
- **Subtle Knife**: Interdimensional blade. Can be **Invoked** to cut through space and instantly sever adjacent enemy lifelines.
- **Grammaton Beretta**: Dual-action cleric firearm supporting Single, 3-round Burst, and Full-Auto firing modes.
- **Acid Spitter**: Bio-organic corrosive projector. Uses environmental acid pools to reload corrosive bio-rounds.
- **Null Pointer**: Experimental glitch weapon. Applies pure `10d1` plasma splash damage directly to visible actors.
- **Phase Device**: Emergency teleportation device. Instantly relocates the user to a clear floor tile away from danger.
- **Large / Small Medpacks**: Field medical kits restoring health up to the maximum hit point threshold.

## 🔧 Modification Packs

Mod packs allow deep customization of weapon and armor attributes:
- **Power Mod (P)**: Increases base damage output and projectile damage dice.
- **Agility Mod (A)**: Reduces firing and movement action energy costs.
- **Bulk Mod (B)**: Expands magazine and ammunition clip capacity.
- **Technical Mod (T)**: Improves accuracy, reduces reload time, or unlocks weapon fire modes.
